Project Idea: Accessible Reading Assistant
A web-based reading assistant designed to help people with low vision, reading difficulties, or limited literacy understand written information more easily. It transforms complex documents into accessible content through text-to-speech, simplified explanations, and language support.
Problem
Many people face difficulties understanding written information because of visual impairments, reading disabilities, or limited reading proficiency.

Important documents, such as school materials, government announcements, medical instructions, and other written information, may contain complex language that is difficult to understand.

For people with low vision, reading printed documents or small text on screens can also be challenging. These barriers can limit access to education, healthcare information, and essential services.
How It Works
1. Upload or Capture Documents
Users can upload a PDF, take a photo of a printed document, or paste text they want to understand.

2. Extract Text Using OCR
The system uses optical character recognition (OCR) to convert text from images and scanned documents into readable digital text.

3. Simplify Difficult Content
AI rewrites complicated passages into simpler language while preserving the original meaning.

4. Listen to the Content
Text-to-speech allows users to listen to the extracted or simplified text instead of reading it.

5. Translate and Explain
Users can translate content into Filipino or another supported language and ask questions about difficult words or passages.

6. Customize Accessibility Settings
Users can adjust font size, contrast, spacing, and reading speed to suit their needs.
Main Goal
To make written information more accessible and understandable for people who experience reading barriers, helping them access education, healthcare information, and essential services more independently.
